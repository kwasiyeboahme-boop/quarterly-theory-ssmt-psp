# Quarterly Theory — Time Cycle Reference

## Overview

Quarterly Theory divides all time cycles into 4 phases (Q1–Q4), creating a hierarchical framework for market structure:

- **Yearly Cycle** (4 quarters across 12 months)
- **Monthly Cycle** (4 weeks per month)
- **Weekly Cycle** (4 days: Mon–Thu)
- **Daily Cycle** (4 sessions: Asia/LO/NY/LC)
- **Intraday Cycles** (90M, Micro — further subdivisions)

Each quarter has distinct characteristics: **Accumulation** → **Manipulation** → **Distribution** → **Close/Reversal**.

## Yearly Cycle

| Quarter | Period | Characteristic | Market Action |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------------|
| Q1 (A/O) | Jan–Apr | Accumulation / Open | Institutional buying, base building |
| Q2 (M/H-L) | Apr–May | Manipulation / High or Low | Fake-outs, liquidity grab, confusion |
| Q3 (D/L-H) | May–Nov | Distribution / Low or High | Institutional selling, range expansion |
| Q4 (C) | Dec | Close / Reversal | Year-end reversals, position unwinding |

**Key Date:** April 1 = True Year Open (resets yearly range)

## Quarter Characteristics

### Q1 — Accumulation / Opening Phase
- **Price action:** Building base, low volatility, basing patterns
- **Volume:** Increasing but steady
- **Trader behavior:** Smart money accumulating, retail buying slowly
- **Setup type:** Breakouts from base, quiet setups
- **Risk:** Fake breakouts early in phase

### Q2 — Manipulation / High or Low Phase
- **Price action:** Chop, oscillation, confusion
- **Volume:** High but directionless
- **Trader behavior:** Retail FOMO buying/selling, institutions shaking out weak hands
- **Setup type:** Range-bound, false breakouts
- **Risk:** Whipsaws, stops hit frequently

### Q3 — Distribution / Directional Phase
- **Price action:** Strong trending move, expansion
- **Volume:** High and directional
- **Trader behavior:** Institutions exiting positions, retail following trend
- **Setup type:** Trend-following, momentum plays
- **Risk:** Late entry, trend exhaustion near end of phase

### Q4 — Close / Reversal Phase
- **Price action:** Reversal, unwinding, consolidation
- **Volume:** Declining or explosive (depending on reversal strength)
- **Trader behavior:** Position closing, profit-taking, institutional unwinding
- **Setup type:** Reversals, breakdowns of highs/lows
- **Risk:** Counter-trend trades fail in early Q1

## SSMT — Sequential SMT (Between Consecutive Quarters)

### Definition
An SMT (Sequential Market Turn) occurring between two **consecutive quarters** where:
- One asset **breaks** the previous quarter high/low (PQH/PQL)
- A correlated asset **fails** to follow
- This divergence signals either **reversal** or **expansion**

### Why It Works
- Each quarter has distinct market structure
- Transition zones (Q1→Q2, Q2→Q3, etc.) concentrate smart money
- Divergence = crack in correlation = structural weakness
- HTF SSMT (Weekly/Monthly/Yearly) = highest confidence

### Example
**Daily Cycle, Q2→Q3 transition:**
- sym_A breaks Q2 high (London Open high)
- sym_B fails to break Q2 high
- = SSMT_BEAR signal (pressure developing)
- PSP confirmation on 15M TF next
- Entry on 5M TF

---

## PSP — Precision Swing Point (Within Active Quarter)

### Definition
A 3-candle swing point where the **middle candle closes opposite** across correlated assets:
- sym_A closes bullish (close > open)
- sym_B closes bearish (close < open)
- = Crack in correlation confirmed
- Must follow valid SSMT

### Why It Works
- Swing points are natural turning points
- Opposite closes = disagreement on direction
- Follows SSMT = already confirmed structure weakness
- Entry TF is next lower timeframe

## Session Priority (UTC)

### Highest Probability Sessions
1. **New York Open (NY)** — Most volatility, liquidity, institutional flow
2. **London Open (LO)** — Overlap with NY, Asian volume continuation
3. **Asia (KZ)** — Risk-off/on flows, beginning of day
4. **Post Meridiem (PM)** — Afternoon, lower vol but key closes
5. **London Close (LC)** — Potential reversals, fund closes

### Session Filters
- Signals fire differently per session
- Q2 (Manipulation phase) = highest whipsaw risk
- Q3 (Distribution phase) = best trend trades
- Tailor position sizing per session/quarter combo

## True Opens (Reference Levels)

### True Week Open
- **Date:** Monday
- **Time:** 18:00 UTC (6pm London time)
- **Price:** Close at this exact time
- **Use:** Weekly reference level, often acts as support/resistance

### True Month Open
- **Date:** 2nd Monday of the month
- **Time:** Open at this exact time
- **Price:** Open at 2nd Monday
- **Use:** Monthly reference level

### True Year Open
- **Date:** April 1
- **Time:** Open at this exact time
- **Price:** Open at April 1
- **Use:** Yearly reference level, resets yearly range

## Implementation in Pine Script

### Quarter Detection
```pine
// Pseudo-code
if month >= 1 and month <= 4
    year_quarter = "Q1"
else if month >= 4 and month <= 5
    year_quarter = "Q2"
else if month >= 5 and month <= 11
    year_quarter = "Q3"
else
    year_quarter = "Q4"
```

### Session Detection
```pine
// Based on hour (UTC)
if hour >= 6 and hour < 12
    session = "Asia"
else if hour >= 12 and hour < 18
    session = "LO"
else if hour >= 18 and hour < 0
    session = "NY"
else
    session = "LC"
```

### Quarter Length
```pine
// Number of bars in prior quarter
quarter_length = 63  // yearly
quarter_length = 30  // monthly (on 4H)
quarter_length = 6   // weekly (on 1H)
quarter_length = 6   // daily (on 15M)
quarter_length = 4   // 90M (on 5M)
```
